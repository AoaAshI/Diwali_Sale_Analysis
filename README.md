# Diwali Sales Analysis

## Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Diwali sales data to understand customer purchasing behavior, identify high-performing product categories, and evaluate key revenue trends. The objective is to derive actionable insights that can support data-driven decision-making for businesses during festive seasons.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing patterns  
- Identify top-performing product categories  
- Evaluate sales distribution across different regions  
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve business performance  

---

## Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)  
- Data Visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn)  
- Jupyter Notebook  

---

## Key Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and preprocessing  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
- Analysis based on gender and age groups  
- State-wise sales analysis  
- Product category performance evaluation  
- Revenue distribution analysis  

---

## Key Insights
- Married women in the age group of 26–35 contributed the highest share of total sales  
- Certain states demonstrated significantly higher purchasing activity compared to others  
- A limited number of product categories accounted for a major portion of total revenue  
- Customer demographics had a strong influence on purchasing behavior  

---

## Recommendations
- Focus marketing efforts on high-value customer segments  
- Prioritize inventory planning for top-performing product categories  
- Develop region-specific strategies based on sales performance trends  

---

- Source: Diwali Sales Dataset (Kaggle / provided dataset)
